==========================================================================
=            MULTI-PLAN TELECOMMUNICATIONS COST OPTIMIZATION             =
==========================================================================
Pack cost = ceil(days / validity) x price; excess usage is charged
per extra GB / minute / SMS beyond the packs that were bought
A plan is eligible when its total cost stays within the budget
